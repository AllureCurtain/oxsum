## Participant observations

Record the participant's own answers, not the observer's preferred explanation:

- What produced the last bill and how would you dispute it?
- What is the difference between a paid renewal and repayment of an older statement?
- When does a refund become confirmed? What does review suspense mean?
- What does the retained proof establish, and what does it not establish?
- Which routine operation was most confusing, and what help was needed?
- Would you continue using the portal/integration? Why or why not?
