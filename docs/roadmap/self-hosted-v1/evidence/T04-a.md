Organization, tenant, external ID and business mapping remain immutable. Profile rename/contact changes leave keys, balance and evidence intact. Archive blocks new gateway/service admission while existing holds settle, payment lands and customer history remains readable. Manual, dunning and archive reasons are independent; clearing one cannot clear another. Lifecycle uses the existing customer admission lock, tested through racing key holds and a real production gateway pointed at a loopback provider.

The pre-0075 fixture checks manual/dunning/active upgrade cases and key mapping.
