## Behavior and boundaries

A version-2 evidence bundle commits an organization's *entire* closed billing
span — the usage records settled in `[from, to]`, the plan statements and
invoice journals they billed through, the payment orders and fulfillments
that paid them, the refunds, disputes and credit notes that adjusted them,
and the day-seal/anchor digests that closed the days they booked on. A
statement-scoped mint (`GET /api/v1/org/statements/{statementId}/evidence`)
commits one document. The commitment is a typed manifest of `{kind, id,
digest}` rows — each artifact digested under `oxsum/evidence-artifact/v1/
<kind>` at mint, the manifest folded under `oxsum/evidence-manifest/v1` —
bound into the tenant ledger by the established self-balancing 1-minor
`Equity:Adjustments` marker whose `DocumentRef` commits
`oxsum/evidence-bundle/v1` → `manifestDigest`. The marker's idempotency key
is `evidence-bundle/<scope>`: one bundle per `(organization, kind, scope)`
ever, and a re-mint replays the standing row. `bundle_json` is stored
bytea-verbatim — every digest commits mint-time serialization, so both the
owner download and the public token download splice the stored bytes inside
`{"data":…}` rather than re-serializing.

`oxsum_verify::verify_evidence_bundle` re-digests every served artifact
against the manifest, refolds the manifest against the committed digest,
checks the marker binds it, adopt-verifies each committed ledger entry into
the signed head, and verifies the head's note under the published key.
`version` dispatches: `2` runs this chain; `1`/absent fails closed as
`WrongVersion` — `verify_usage_report` still owns the legacy semantics, and
the v1 consumer checks pass unchanged. Every rejection names its step
(`Malformed`, `ScopeMismatch`, `MissingArtifact`, `ArtifactMismatch`,
`ManifestMismatch`, `MarkerMismatch`, `EntryHash`, `Inclusion`, `Unsigned`,
`WrongOrigin`, `HeadMismatch`, `BadSignature`). The wasm-bindgen surface
exports it as `verifyEvidenceBundle`; `verify-oxsum-bill` wraps it with the
shared published-key normalization.

Share tokens (`oxe-…`) are bearer capabilities with a spendable budget:
SHA-256 stored, plaintext answered once at mint, `expires_at` (30 days
default, 90 max), an optional `maxDownloads` cap spent atomically inside
the resolving UPDATE, and `DELETE …/share` stamping `revoked_at` on every
live share. `GET /api/v1/evidence/{token}` resolves without a session;
expired, revoked, exhausted and unknown tokens answer the same `410` so a
guessed token leaks nothing, while a storage failure answers `500` — a
`410` would falsely report the token dead. `download_count` and
`last_download_at` audit every resolution. All `/org/evidence-bundles*` and
`/org/statements/{id}/evidence` routes are `owner-admin` sessions and deny
consumption keys: a bundle exports the organization's whole billing span.

The `scope` block is the honest half: `covered` names the committed classes
(`usageRecords`, `planStatements`, `invoiceJournals`, `paymentOrders`,
`refunds`, `disputes`, `creditNotes`, `daySeals`, `ledgerEntries`);
`excluded` names what the bundle does not answer for — the platform's own
supplier-cost figures, another organization's rows, history outside the
span, in-flight operations. `upstreamCostMinor`/`real_attempt` redact by
default (the platform's own cost ledger); `OXSUM_EVIDENCE_UPSTREAM_COST`
opts them in and `scope.upstreamCostIncluded` records which case ran.
`costBasis` always exports.

The A10/A26 matrix: the genuine minted bundle verifies end to end over HTTP;
tampered artifact figures (`ArtifactMismatch`), a dropped manifest row
(`ManifestMismatch`), tampered marker/commitment (`MarkerMismatch`), wrong
organization (`Malformed`), wrong origin (`WrongOrigin`), wrong operator key
(`BadSignature`), entry-hash and inclusion tampering, the unsigned-
deployment path and share expiry/revocation/budget each fail by name.
Historical-name and unanchored-history changes surface as manifest or entry
digest mismatches. Supplier cost stays redacted unless the env flag opts
in — asserted by `the_usage_artifact_redacts_upstream_cost_by_default`.

## Known boundaries

- The proof is over the committed set: an organization that never wrote a
  row has nothing to drop, and the bundle cannot enumerate rows outside
  `scope.covered` — the honest exclusions are spelled in `scope.excluded`
  and proofs.md's row.
- `verifyEvidenceBundle` reads the ledger origin out of the bundle's own
  signed note (the same choice `verifyUsageReport` makes): a bundle minted
  elsewhere answers under its own origin rather than silently inheriting a
  caller-supplied one.
- The public download is a bearer capability by design — the token is the
  credential, never indexed, and its download count is the audit a "who
  fetched this" question reads.
- The committed `crates/web/verify-wasm/` artifacts remain placeholders;
  `scripts/build-verify-wasm.sh` produces the real bundle at image build
  time, the same trade-off `web/dist/index.html` takes.
