# T03.b — Business staff capabilities and selected session contexts

Task: #630, child of #594. Scenarios: A01, A04, A06. Candidate: `ed58c3e` on `feat/business-staff`, based on T03.a `b51d440`. Documentation follows in the same PR. Environment: local isolated PostgreSQL 17, scripted OAuth/OIDC providers; no live provider/payment/deployment action.

## Implemented behavior

- Explicit customer/business session selection, with deny-by-default core role capabilities. Every business operation rechecks the live secret/session/current staff role; mutations hold the business lock through resource changes, immutable idempotency receipts and actor-aware audit.
- Owner-controlled existing-user appointment/revocation, atomic transfer, last-owner account-deletion refusal, unchanged independent customer memberships. Staff can log in after losing all customer seats, then explicitly select business context. Keys and deployment tokens cannot act as staff.
- Fresh/upgraded registration remains invite-only until the initialized owner saves open policy. Public password and both OAuth providers use the stored policy under a transaction lock; legacy environment values do not override it. Signup creates no staff seat.
- Context isolation applies through the existing customer resolver, including bill export and WebSocket. Personal security/account settings accept identity in either context without granting customer or staff privileges.
- Password/reset/TOTP lifecycle uses a common lock order. The verified password hash is rechecked before session/challenge creation; challenge/backup-code consumption and session creation commit atomically. Reset kills pending challenges and sessions. Confirmed TOTP cannot be overwritten through enrollment; business use requires the session's second factor when enabled.
- Legacy unrelated HTTP fixtures explicitly set open policy in scratch-only databases. No test bypass is added to the production router or business policy code.
